DeltaOdyssey

Version 2.20

Drop your Delta disk image here (the .d64, or the .zip it came in). The game reads the original sprites and Rob Hubbard's music straight from your disk. Nothing is uploaded.

Touch: drag anywhere on the screen to fly. Tap to choose. Buttons for auto fire, weapon, bomb and power shield appear in levels.
Player 1: arrows, Z or Space fire, X switch weapon, C bomb, V take power-up, B shift points between speed and twin shot, N power shield.
Player 2: WASD, F fire, G switch weapon, H bomb, J take power-up, K shift, L power shield. Gamepads work too.
Esc pauses. M turns music on or off.
